The workbook contains protein identifiers, functional annotations, sequence and source information, screening decisions, and member-to-representative mappings. The 611 functional candidates map to 575 representatives (36 redundant entries merged). Source organism and test-category fields distinguish protein provenance from experimental strain coverage. Entries are predicted interaction-scoring targets, not experimentally established peptide binding targets. No experimental peptide dataset is included.

Source: UniProt records and archived functional-screening outputs. Selection did not use peptide MIC outcomes.
